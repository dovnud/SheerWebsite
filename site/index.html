Sheer Davidai — Producer, Director & Choreographer

PRODUCER | DIRECTOR & CHOREOGRAPHER

Sheer Davidai is a producer, director and choreographer, and the Producing Artistic Director of ROUGE at The STRAT and WOW — The Vegas Spectacular at the Rio in Las Vegas. The two productions play fifteen to twenty shows a week, with more than eighty artists and staff, on combined annual budgets of over $8M. Neither show is licensed material. Both were developed in-house, through workshops, readings and revision, until the work was ready to meet an audience.

She came to producing from inside the performance. From 2002 to 2019 she toured the world as a performer and dance captain, thirteen of those years as a lead dancer with the Sheketak ensemble, appearing in more than thirty countries and at Eurovision. From 2006 she was also building other people's worlds, as associate director and choreographer on the WOW franchise, Aqua Circus and Rome Circus, and by 2018 she led artistic development and producing outright.

As Producing Artistic Director of Extravaganza (2020 to 2023), she wrote the safety protocols that kept the production performing through COVID while other large American productions went dark, a story national television told through Inside Edition in 2021. She holds the EB-1A for extraordinary ability in the arts and Best of Las Vegas awards.

Every production she runs began the same way: a blank page, a company of artists, and the patience to build it until it was alive.
